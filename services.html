Frequently Asked Questions

How fast is delivery?

Most small and medium projects are delivered in 1–3 business days. Larger or more complex jobs usually take 5–7 days. You always receive a clear timeline before work starts.

What file formats do you deliver?

CSV, Excel (XLSX), JSON or Google Sheets — whichever you prefer. Data is cleaned and ready to use.

Is the data legal to use?

I only collect publicly available information and follow responsible practices. You remain responsible for how you use the data under applicable laws (email outreach rules, GDPR, etc.).

How do I pay?

PayPal, Wise or bank transfer. Small projects are usually paid in full. Larger projects can be split (50% advance).

Can you handle ongoing / recurring jobs?

Yes. Many clients need weekly or monthly updates of the same data sources. We can set up a simple recurring schedule.

Don’t see the exact service you need?

I handle custom scraping projects every week. Message me with the website, the fields you need and the volume.
