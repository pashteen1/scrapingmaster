TOOL COMPARISONS

BeautifulSoup vs Selenium vs Playwright

Choosing the right tool saves weeks of frustration. BeautifulSoup is perfect for static HTML. Selenium and Playwright are needed when the page depends on JavaScript.

Key points

  • Start with the simplest tool that can solve the problem.
  • Always respect rate limits and the site’s terms.
  • Clean and validate data before you use it.
  • Log errors and keep checkpoints for long jobs.

Practical advice

Most scraping projects fail because of poor planning, not because of missing advanced techniques. Define the exact fields you need, test on a small sample, then scale carefully.
